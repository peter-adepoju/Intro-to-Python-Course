Privacy

What Python Atlas keeps about you, where it's kept, and how to remove it. Last updated 26 October 2026.

Without an account

Everything you do (finished days, exercise answers, quiz results, review cards, settings and playground scripts) is saved only in your own browser. Nothing is sent to us. Clearing your browser's site data removes it.

With an account

When you create an account, we store:

  • your email address and, if you give it, your name;
  • if you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture from your Google account;
  • your learning progress: the same items listed above, so they appear on every device you use.

This is stored by our database provider, Supabase. Your password is never visible to us. Each learner can only read and change their own progress.

The AI tutor

When you ask the tutor a question, we send your question, the name of the lesson you're on, and any code or error you chose to share to an AI service (Google Gemini) to write an answer. Please don't include personal information in tutor questions. On the free service, Google may use these messages to improve its products.

Running your code

Your Python code runs inside your own browser. It isn't sent anywhere to be run.

What we never do

  • We don't sell or share your information with advertisers.
  • We don't show ads or use tracking cookies.

Your choices

  • Download everything we hold about your learning from your account page.
  • To delete your account and all its data, email petera@aims.ac.za from the address you signed up with. We'll delete it within 30 days.
